Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type and general usage patterns, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and are only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

You may delete your expense records or request deletion of your account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the Service. These providers only process data as necessary to support Spendly's functionality and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We do not share your personal expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
